## 👋 About Me

I'm a **B.E. Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology)** student at **Chennai Institute of Technology** (2023–2027), with a **9.26 CGPA**.

I enjoy taking an idea all the way from **RTL → synthesis → timing → physical layout**, and I'm especially drawn to:

- 🔋 **Low-power VLSI** and emerging devices (CNTFET / hybrid logic)
- 🔐 **Hardware security**: post-quantum crypto and lightweight ciphers in silicon
- 🧠 **ML-assisted hardware**: smart power management on FPGAs
- 🧪 **Analog / custom IC design** in advanced nodes (14nm FinFET)

> 🎯 **Open to:** VLSI / RTL design, physical design, verification, and research internship opportunities.
